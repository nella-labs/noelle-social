# Code of Conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Different experience levels and viewpoints are welcome.

Discuss the work with clear evidence. Give useful feedback, accept corrections, and respect another person's time and privacy. Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual comments, personal attacks, impersonation and publication of private information are unacceptable. Do not use project spaces to promote unsolicited outreach or abuse social accounts.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, close a discussion, restrict participation or ban an account. The response should fit the behavior and its impact.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). If no private contact is available, open an issue requesting one without identifying affected people or publishing the incident details.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests and other project spaces.
